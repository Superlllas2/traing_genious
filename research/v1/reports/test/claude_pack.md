About Ilia and the app:
Ilia: technical background, no formal finance training. He is designing an AI market insight app for stocks. The app reads news and research over weeks, links events into storylines (this happened, so that should follow; when the expected next event appears, the link is confirmed) and helps a human trader decide with less emotion. It is not high frequency trading and not based on price patterns. The goal is to beat the habits of casual retail traders, not to compete with funds. This review is about methods: what to actually trade when trading on news and events over days to weeks, how long to hold, what it returns after costs, and whether it still works. Screening rules. Keep: stocks in any country (US and Europe preferred), real market data, studies that report returns, holding periods or trading costs. Prefer: open full text (NBER, SSRN and arXiv versions count), samples that run past 2010, and applied journals such as the Financial Analysts Journal and the Journal of Portfolio Management. Drop (relevance 2 or less): technical analysis and chart patterns, high frequency and intraday trading, crypto, forex, options strategies, and pure theory without data.
